=== KDE Store – Name ===
SeaQ SoundWave

=== Summary (one line) ===
Audio visualizer with 19 styles, an orb or spinning record that pauses your music & videos, panel / fixed / free placement and 10 languages.

=== Description (paste into the store; the store understands BBCode) ===
[b]SeaQ SoundWave[/b] turns whatever you hear into light – right on your Plasma 6 desktop.

[b]✨ Highlights[/b]
[list]
[*][b]19 styles in 4 groups[/b] – Classic, macOS (colour waves, LED matrix, skyline, rainbow bars), Lines & Light (double helix, laser fan, light strings, colour streams, silk waves) and Round (ring spectrum, segment ring, pulse rings, liquid orb, kaleidoscope)
[*][b]The orb[/b] – effects flow out of a small orb in the middle. Click it to pause or resume audio [i]and[/i] video (YouTube, Udemy, VLC, Spotify …). One switch for all styles
[*][b]NEW: the record[/b] – a vinyl record as the orb: spins with the music, a tone arm swings on and off, the cover of the current song sits on the label
[*][b]Place it anywhere[/b] – inside the widget, attached to a panel (top, bottom, left, right; centred, 1 px gap by default), at one of nine fixed points, or completely free
[*][b]Free positioning[/b] – Meta+Alt+V unlocks, drag and resize on every edge and corner, Meta+Alt+V locks again. The widget itself stays invisible
[*][b]Pop-up card[/b] – right click the visualizer for preview, pause, lock and settings; click an empty spot to close it
[*][b]Colour collection[/b] – 26 palettes plus a gradient editor for your own colours, with flowing colour animation
[*][b]Settings editor[/b] – clean System-Settings look, every style only shows the options it really uses, reset buttons everywhere and a factory reset
[*][b]Audio protection[/b] – never leaves other apps muted; finds muted apps and repairs them with one click
[*][b]10 languages[/b] – English, Deutsch, Français, Español, Italiano, Nederlands, हिन्दी, 简体中文, 日本語, 한국어
[/list]

[b]🚀 How to use[/b]
[list=1]
[*]Install, then right click the desktop → Add Widgets… → SeaQ SoundWave
[*]Right click the widget → Configure SeaQ SoundWave… → pick style, palette and placement
[*]Open the settings editor for everything else
[/list]

[b]📦 Requirements[/b]
python3 and parec (pulseaudio-utils) or pw-record (pipewire-utils). cava is used when installed. The orb uses MPRIS via busctl (part of systemd).

[b]🌍 Translations[/b]
If the language doesn't switch after installing from the store, run the install.sh from GitHub once or restart Plasma.
