## 💿 SeaQ SoundWave 2.5.0 – the record orb

![Banner](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/SeaQSoft/SeaQ-SoundWave/main/docs/banner.png)

### ✨ What's new
- 💿 **New orb style “Record”** – a vinyl record that spins with the music (33 rpm) and stops on pause
- 🎚️ **Tone arm** swings onto the record when playing and away on pause
- 🖼️ **Cover on the label** – the cover of the song or video that is playing right now
- 🔁 **One orb for all styles** – on/off, look, colour and play/pause apply to every style at once (only the size stays per style)
- ✨ The light in the middle is never cut off at the edge anymore
- 🌍 All new texts in 10 languages

### 🎵 Also included from 2.4.1
- Effects react to the music again (capture stream fix) and move much more with volume and bass
- The orb on the desktop really pauses and resumes music and video
- Continuous lines without the orb, light in the middle for all Lines & Light styles

### 📦 Install / update
```sh
kpackagetool6 -t Plasma/Applet -i SeaQ-SoundWave-2.5.0.plasmoid   # first install
kpackagetool6 -t Plasma/Applet -u SeaQ-SoundWave-2.5.0.plasmoid   # update
systemctl --user restart plasma-plasmashell
```
